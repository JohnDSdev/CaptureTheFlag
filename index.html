01 — Overview

THE BASICS

01

Two Teams

Six players per side — Team Red vs Team Black. Each team has a captain who coordinates strategy and role distribution before the game begins.

02

Three Roles

Each team fields two Guardians, two Runners, and two Hunters. Roles are assigned before the game starts. Choose wisely — they can't be changed mid-game.

03

Two Flags Each

Each team protects two flags at separate bases. Each base can hold a maximum of two flags at once — your own plus one captured.

04

Card Duels

Confrontations between players are resolved through a card game. Your card hand is your lifeline — lose too many and you become vulnerable.

Win Condition

Capture both of the opposing team's flags and secure them equally across your two bases. One flag per base. First team to achieve this wins.

02 — Roles

KNOW YOUR
POSITION

🛡️
Guardian
2 per team

The last line of defense. A Guardian's world is their flag — they stay with it at all times, watching and waiting for runners to dare approach.

  • Stays at their assigned flag at all times
  • If their flag is taken, relocates to guard the remaining flag
  • Can tag and capture both Runners AND Hunters who enter the base
  • Only role with the ability to take down Hunters
🏃
Runner
2 per team

Speed and stealth. Runners are the offensive backbone of the team — they venture into enemy territory and bring flags home.

  • Primary goal: capture enemy flags and return them to your base
  • Can only carry one flag at a time
  • Each base can hold max two flags (your own + one captured)
  • Vulnerable to Hunters in open field
🎯
Hunter
2 per team

The enforcer. Hunters roam the field targeting enemy Runners, stopping captures before they happen through card game duels.

  • Tags and initiates card duels with enemy Runners
  • If Hunter wins duel: Runner is escorted to a base as a prisoner
  • Prisoner must stay inactive until freed by a teammate tag
  • Freeing teammate requires the savior to have at least 2 cards

Team Distribution

One team plays all black cards, the other all red. A full deck of 52 cards is split evenly — 26 per team.

4 Aces set aside with the flags — not in player hands
24 Cards distributed among the 6 players
3+2 Cards per player: 3 number cards + 2 specials (J, Q, K)

How Duels Work

Both players secretly choose a card, then reveal simultaneously. Higher card wins. Ties are replayed until there's a winner. The winner takes any one card they want from the loser.

The Aces

Each ace is stored with a flag. When a Runner captures that flag, they also acquire the ace — a one-time automatic win card. Use it wisely. After use, it functions as a 1 (the lowest possible card).

Running Out of Cards

No cards = instant loss if tagged. You cannot be saved by a teammate unless they have at least 2 cards to spare — and they must hand you one upon freeing you.

04 — Special Rule

THE CHAOS
CLAUSE

When all of a team's flags have been taken from their bases — but haven't been secured in enemy bases yet — the game enters a frantic scramble mode.

Every player on the losing team drops their assigned role and becomes a Runner/Hunter hybrid, doing whatever it takes to reclaim at least one flag.

Roles return to normal the moment they successfully win a duel against a flag carrier and recover a flag.

1 Both flags are taken from your bases but not yet secured at enemy bases
2 All players on your team become Runner/Hunter hybrids instantly
3 Win a duel against a flag carrier to reclaim the flag
4 Roles return to normal once a flag is recovered

05 — Communication

TEAM COMMS

Players will have access to real-time text communication during the game. This is a massive strategic advantage — use it.

📍

Flag Intel

The moment a runner grabs a flag, alert the whole team so Guardians can reposition and Hunters can run interference on their path home.

🔒

Prisoner Tracking

Hunters should broadcast which base they're escorting prisoners to, so teammates know exactly where to go for the rescue.

⚡

Chaos Calls

If the Chaos Clause kicks in, coordinate a counter immediately through the gc instead of everyone panicking and improvising solo.

🎖️

Captain's Command

The captain should act as a field general — tracking the situation and issuing coordinated plays rather than just playing their own role.

06 — Strategy

HOW TO
ACTUALLY WIN

Double Up on One Flag First

Send both Runners after the same flag to begin with. Get it secured at a base, then go for the second. Splitting up is how you lose flags you already captured.

Hunters Should Intercept, Not Camp

Shadow enemy Runners aggressively in the field. Waiting at base is reactive and slow — catch them mid-run before they reach your flags.

Card Economy is Everything

Don't blow high cards early, but don't hoard so hard that you lose duels and bleed cards anyway. Losing cards is essentially losing health.

Save the Ace for a Critical Moment

An ace is irresistible, but using it early turns it into a worthless 1. Hold it for a duel that actually matters — a flag on the line, a prisoner break, a last stand.

Card-Check Before Rescues

Only attempt a prisoner rescue if you have at least 2 cards. A failed or card-broke rescue attempt just adds another prisoner to the pile.

Guardians Are More Offensive Than You Think

Guardians can tag Hunters too — which is uniquely powerful. Use them aggressively when enemies enter the base, don't just stand there watching.
